## Overview

Created to learn the fundamentals of PCB design in KiCad, build mechanical CAD skills in FreeCAD by designing a 3D-printed enclosure, and assemble a practical device. Inspired by DigiKey's KiCad course on YouTube with modifications to the circuit.

## Components

* **Power & Switching:** 9 V battery, 2N7000 MOSFET, tactile switch, screw terminal
* **Indicators & Alerts:** Buzzer, LED
* **Passives & Hardware:** 10 MΩ resistor, 6.8 kΩ resistor, 1 nF capacitor, heat-set inserts, M3 screws

## Tools Used

* **KiCad:** Schematic capture and PCB layout
* **FreeCAD:** Enclosure design for a 3D-printed housing
* **GitHub:** Version control and project documentation

## What I Learned

* Creating schematics in KiCad
* PCB component placement and trace routing
* Designing and 3D-printing an enclosure fitted to a custom PCB
